# Code of Conduct

AutoForge is committed to a welcoming, professional and harassment-free
community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience or
level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive and specific when giving feedback.
- Assume good intent while still addressing harmful impact.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's credentials or private
  project data.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, moderation and project direction.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
doxxing, spam and deliberate disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, harmful or
unsafe. Enforcement decisions will consider context, severity and repeated
behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately to a project maintainer using the contact
information on their GitHub profile. If the concern involves a maintainer or
immediate safety, use GitHub's confidential reporting and abuse channels.
Reports will be handled as privately as practical; retaliation against a
reporter or witness is prohibited.

This code applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions
and other spaces where someone is representing the AutoForge project.
